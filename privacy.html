What this website does

This pre-launch website provides brand information and contact links for SCHefferz. It does not currently process online orders or payments, and it does not send form submissions to a website database.

When you contact us

If you email us directly, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and enquiry. We use it to respond and manage the conversation.

Forms and email links

The enquiry and newsletter forms on this version open your device’s email application. They are not a hosted mailing-list service and do not automatically store submissions on this website.

Third-party services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and links to Instagram. Those services may process technical data according to their own policies when you visit or follow a link.

Future updates

Before online sales, analytics, mailing-list storage or payment processing are enabled, this policy must be updated to describe the services actually used and the choices available to visitors.

Contact
