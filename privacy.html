Privacy
policy.

A plain-language overview of how this pre-launch website handles information.

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Information you provide

Contact and wholesale forms on this static preview site open your default email application with the information you enter. The website itself does not store those form submissions in a database.

Shopping and analytics

The current cart uses browser local storage to remember demo cart items on your device. Checkout is not enabled and no payment information is collected by this website. Analytics and advertising tracking are not intentionally configured in this build.

External services

Links to external services such as Instagram may be subject to their own privacy policies. If the site later adds newsletter tools, analytics, payments or order management, this policy must be reviewed and updated before launch.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact hello@schefferz.com.
